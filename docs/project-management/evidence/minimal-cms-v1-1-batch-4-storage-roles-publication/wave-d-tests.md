# Batch 4 wave D — tests

| Test | What it proves |
| --- | --- |
| `PublicationLifecycleTest` | Nothing published until something publishes it; the head reads back; a newer revision replaces the head and the old one is remembered; **one locale published while another is a draft**; one scope published while another is not; unpublish clearing the head and naming what it withdrew; archive stamping its time; a record brought back from the archive; explain listing the transitions legal from here, and a never-published record unable to be unpublished |
| `PublicationTransitionMatrixTest` | All twenty cells of the matrix, each refused cell reporting `invalid_transition`; an illegal transition writing neither a state row nor a log row; a schedule setting no head; a schedule over a published record leaving the live head alone |
| `PublicationHistoryTest` | Five transitions in order with the from and to state of each; the revision recorded where meaningful and null where not; the actor of each transition; **no field value anywhere in the log**; the log append-only, with existing ids unchanged after a new transition; history per record, scope and locale; the limit honoured and required positive |
| `PublicationSweepTest` | Only due schedules fire; the scheduled revision is what gets published; the schedule is cleared once it fires; a future schedule is untouched; the log says `sweep_publish` by the sweep's actor; a second sweep is a no-op that appends nothing; time moving forward brings the future schedule due; the limit stops the batch and the rest fires next run; `dueSchedules()` reading without publishing |
| `PublicationFailsClosedTest` | An unknown revision refused with the head left alone; an invalid revision on publish and schedule; three malformed locales; **publishing changing no localized value, asserted byte for byte**; `schema_missing` on head, publish and sweep; and an unbound revision check meaning the caller took responsibility, stated rather than pretended |
