| Test | What it proves |
| --- | --- |
| `ReadContractsResolveKeyTest` | An unpublished record does not resolve; a published one does with its public fields and without its admin field; a miss is a miss; another scope and another locale are different questions; a localized value wins for its locale while the other is untouched; unpublishing and scheduling both stop the key resolving; **two candidates fail closed with `ambiguous_key`**; a filtered record resolves to nothing at all |

| `SlugUniquenessTest` | A taken slug refused and the holder named; a free slug available; **another scope and another locale may reuse it**; a record keeping its own slug; another record still unable to take it; unpublishing freeing it and republishing taking it back; a second holder seen even when the first is excluded |
| `ReadContractsFailsClosedTest` | A schema nobody declared projecting nothing rather than its whole document — **including its localized values**; a role reference with no active binding failing closed; three malformed locales; two invalid budgets; a newer unpublished revision never reaching a reader; `schema_missing` on all three entry points; and the shared values still projecting without a localized reader bound |
| `RoleBoundSiteTreeTwoLocalesTest` | The `role_bound_site_tree_two_locales` acceptance criterion, end to end: role binding, one record set, one tree, two locales, same ids and revisions in both renders, shared non-localized fields, admin fields excluded, the tree navigable in both locales, `resolveKey` per locale against the same slugs, coverage complete, and an untranslated node still rendering with its shared value |
